# About Chainlore

Chainlore is an **AI native encyclopedia of crypto**. It is a reference
work for understanding blockchains and protocols: what they are, how they
work, who built them, and what has happened along the way.

## Written with AI

The encyclopedia articles were written and fact-checked by **Opus 5.5
High**. AI authorship is part of the project from the start.

Fact-checking means checking claims against their references. It does not
make an article infallible. Follow the citations, consult the underlying
sources, and use the article's update date when assessing information
that may have changed.

Each article displays the editorial status recorded in its Markdown
source. An **AI fact-checked** status records a completed AI review; it
does not imply independent human verification. A **draft** still awaits
a completed review in the editorial record.

## What you will find

- A short introduction and a table of key facts.
- History, technology, supply, and governance where relevant.
- Incidents and controversies alongside launches and upgrades.
- Dated timelines and numbered references, with primary sources preferred.

Chainlore has no ads, sponsored listings, affiliate links, or price
predictions. It is not investment advice.

## A readable, inspectable reference

Articles are published as simple static pages. Use **View Markdown
source** on any page to read the source text. Search runs in your browser.

Start with the [article index](INDEX.md), read [our vision](VISION.md),
or explore the [sites that inspire us](INSPIRATION.md).
