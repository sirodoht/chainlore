# About Chainlore

Chainlore is an AI native encyclopedia of crypto. Articles cover the
history, technology, supply, governance, and incidents of blockchains
and protocols.

## Authorship and review

The encyclopedia articles were written and fact-checked by Opus 5.5 High.

Each article displays an update date and one of three editorial
statuses from its Markdown source:

- Draft: written, but not yet fully fact-checked. May contain errors.
- AI fact-checked: every claim checked against live sources by an AI
  that did not write the text. The article shows the check date.
- Human reviewed: AI fact-checked, then read, spot-checked, and
  approved by a named reviewer. The article shows the reviewer and date.

The [editorial policy](POLICY.md) sets out what each status requires and
the source rules every article must meet.

AI fact-checking does not constitute independent human verification.

## Article structure

- A short introduction and a table of key facts.
- History, technology, supply, and governance where relevant.
- Incidents and controversies alongside launches and upgrades.
- Dated timelines and numbered references, with primary sources preferred.

## Source files

Articles are stored in Markdown and published as static HTML. Each page
links to its Markdown source. Search runs in the browser.

See the [article index](INDEX.md), [editorial policy](POLICY.md),
[editorial scope](VISION.md), and [reference sites](INSPIRATION.md).
