# Mt. Gox

Mt. Gox was a Japanese [Bitcoin](bitcoin.md) exchange that stopped operating
in February 2014 after reporting missing customer and company funds. Its
collapse led to insolvency proceedings and a prolonged process of returning
assets to creditors. Bitcoin and Bitcoin Cash repayments began in July
2024; some repayments remained outstanding in October 2026.[^filing][^repayment][^deadline]

## Collapse and missing bitcoins

In its 28 February 2014 filing announcement, Mt. Gox said roughly 750,000
customer bitcoins and 100,000 company bitcoins were missing. It also
reported discrepancies in cash balances and said it had closed its website
on 25 February. The announcement's explanation involving a Bitcoin bug was
the company's preliminary account, issued while investigations were
continuing.[^filing]

On 20 March, the company revised the picture: rescanning an old wallet
had located approximately 200,000 BTC on 7 March. It consequently estimated
missing bitcoins at about 650,000, while warning that the amount and causes
remained under investigation. The original 850,000 figure therefore should
not be read as the final unrecovered loss.[^recovery]

## Criminal investigation

In June 2023, the US Department of Justice announced charges against Alexey
Bilyuchenko and Aleksandr Verner. Prosecutors alleged that they gained
access to Mt. Gox's wallet server in 2011 and, with others, caused the theft
of approximately 647,000 BTC through at least May 2014. The announcement
concerned allegations in an indictment, rather than a finding of guilt.
Its account of a multiyear theft is distinct from the exchange's initial
public explanation in February 2014.[^doj]

## Creditor repayments

The rehabilitation trustee announced repayments in Bitcoin and Bitcoin
Cash to some creditors through designated exchanges on 5 July 2024.
Further distributions depended on account validation, arrangements with
exchanges, and checks that transfers could proceed securely.[^repayment]

As of 31 October 2026, the latest deadline notice, dated 16 October,
extended the Base Repayment, Early Lump-Sum Repayment, and Intermediate
Repayment deadlines to 31 October 2027, Japan time. The trustee said these
repayments were largely complete for creditors whose procedures were
finished and problem-free, with exceptions. Other creditors had unfinished
procedures or encountered processing issues. This extension is not a
statement that every creditor must wait until that date.[^deadline]
