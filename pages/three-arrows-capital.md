# Three Arrows Capital

Three Arrows Capital, commonly called 3AC, was an investment firm
founded in 2012 by Su Zhu and Kyle Davies that became known for
cryptocurrency investing. Its British Virgin Islands entity, Three
Arrows Capital Ltd, entered court-ordered liquidation on 27 June 2022.
The collapse exposed substantial loans made to the firm by other
cryptocurrency businesses.[^background][^liquidators][^voyager]

## Investment losses

The collapse of Terra's LUNA token in May 2022 damaged one of 3AC's
investments. In June, Davies told the Wall Street Journal that the firm
had invested $200 million in LUNA and had been caught off guard by the
Terra collapse, according to The Block's account of that interview.
He said the firm was considering asset sales, a rescue, and agreements
with creditors.[^background]

This account describes one exposure rather than a complete balance
sheet. A different part of the crisis was borrowing: on 27 June 2022,
Voyager Digital announced that its operating subsidiary had issued
3AC a default notice on a loan comprising 15,250 BTC and 350 million
USDC. Voyager said required payments had not been made and that it
would pursue recovery.[^voyager]

The loan's two denominations matter. Its bitcoin component changed
value with the market, while the USDC component was a separate token
obligation. Treating a contemporaneous dollar estimate as the permanent
size of the debt would obscure that distinction.[^voyager]

## Liquidation

The joint liquidators' official site records the 27 June 2022 order
of the Eastern Caribbean Supreme Court in the British Virgin Islands
and the appointment of Russell Crumpler and Christopher Farmer. It
provides claim forms and notices for creditors, with additional
materials restricted to approved creditors. A liquidation appointment
starts a recovery and claims process; it does not itself establish
that creditors have been repaid.[^liquidators]

## Singapore enforcement

The Monetary Authority of Singapore's September 2023 announcement
concerned the related Singapore company, Three Arrows Capital Pte Ltd,
where Zhu had been chief executive and Davies chairman. MAS described
its earlier reprimand for false information, notification failures,
and exceeding the assets-under-management limit applicable to its
registration.[^mas]

Following further investigation, MAS said the company had failed to
notify it of a representative's employment, provided false information
about that work, and lacked an appropriate risk-management framework
for cryptocurrency investments. MAS held the two directors responsible
for those compliance failures.[^mas]

The resulting nine-year prohibition orders took effect on 13 September
2023. They barred the founders from regulated activities and specified
management, directorship, and substantial-shareholding roles in capital
market services firms under Singapore's Securities and Futures Act.
These regulatory findings concern particular legal duties, distinct
from the separate liquidation process and creditors' recovery
claims.[^mas][^liquidators]
