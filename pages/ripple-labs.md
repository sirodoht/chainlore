## History

Wikipedia reports that McCaleb left his active role at the company in July 2013 and co-founded the non-profit Stellar Development Foundation with Joyce Kim in 2014.[^6] This is also a weak source.

## Technology

Wikipedia reports that development of the XRP Ledger began in 2011, by David Schwartz, Jed McCaleb and Arthur Britto.[^2] This is a weak source.

## XRP token and business

- One secondary source says Ripple's payments, custody, stablecoin and tokenisation products can work without XRP.[^13] Another says banks can use RippleNet to transfer money worldwide using XRP as a bridge currency.[^14] The two sources differ in emphasis.

## Governance

- Fortress and Citadel Securities led the November 2025 investment.[^1]
