Ripple Labs is a San Francisco-based financial technology company. Its November 2025 release, datelined San Francisco, describes Ripple as offering crypto solutions for businesses.[^imp-49-1] Ripple says its solutions leverage the XRP Ledger, the digital asset XRP and the stablecoin Ripple USD (RLUSD).[^imp-49-2]

## History

Sources differ on how the operating company was founded. Bloomberg's profile of Chris Larsen says he co-founded OpenCoin, which later became known as Ripple, a few months after leaving Prosper in 2012, alongside Jed McCaleb, David Schwartz and Arthur Britto.[^imp-49-3] A weak ownership explainer instead dates the founding of the operating company to September 2012, with McCaleb, Britto and Larsen as founders.[^imp-49-4] Wikipedia's Ripple Labs article says the company was established as NewCoin in September 2012 and renamed OpenCoin the following month.[^imp-49-5] The September 2012 date in the infobox and timeline rests on the weak ownership explainer. The 2013 rename and the 2015 brand change have not been verified against a primary record.

Wikipedia reports that McCaleb left his active role at the company in July 2013 and co-founded the non-profit Stellar Development Foundation with Joyce Kim in 2014.[^6] This is also a weak source.

## Technology

Wikipedia reports that development of the XRP Ledger began in 2011, by David Schwartz, Jed McCaleb and Arthur Britto.[^2] This is a weak source.

Ripple's developer documentation on consensus protections says validators influence only the servers configured to trust them. By default, XRP Ledger servers use recommended validator lists, also called recommended Unique Node Lists (UNLs), published by the XRPL Foundation and Ripple.[^imp-49-6] According to the same page, consensus can continue without problems while less than about 20% of trusted validators are misbehaving at a given time. If more than about 20% are unreachable or not behaving properly, the network fails to reach consensus.[^imp-49-6] The page's 80% figure concerns confirming an invalid transaction, not halting the network.[^imp-49-6]

## XRP token and business

- One secondary source says Ripple's payments, custody, stablecoin and tokenisation products can work without XRP.[^13] Another says banks can use RippleNet to transfer money worldwide using XRP as a bridge currency.[^14] The two sources differ in emphasis.

## Governance

- Fortress and Citadel Securities led the November 2025 investment.[^1]

## Valuation

Ripple announced a $500 million strategic investment at a $40 billion valuation on 5 November 2025, from institutional investors led by funds managed by affiliates of Fortress Investment Group and Citadel Securities.[^imp-49-12][^imp-49-13] The company said it decided to accept $500 million in new common equity, and that the investment followed a recent $1 billion tender offer at the same valuation.[^imp-49-12]
