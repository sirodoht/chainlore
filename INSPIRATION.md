# Inspiration

Sites we learn from, what each does well, and what we leave behind.

## Encyclopedias

| Site | What it is | What we take | What we leave |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org) | General encyclopedia | Neutral tone, lead plus infobox, inline citations | Notability gatekeeping that excludes most of crypto |
| [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com) | AI-written encyclopedia from xAI | AI-written from the start, plain layout | |
| [IQ.wiki](https://iq.wiki) | Crypto encyclopedia, formerly Everipedia | Proof that a crypto-specific wiki is wanted | Project-friendly tone, visual clutter, its own token |
| [Bitcoin Wiki](https://en.bitcoin.it) | Community wiki on Bitcoin | Technical depth, primary sources | Single-subject scope, uneven upkeep |

## Market data aggregators

| Site | What it is | What we take | What we leave |
|---|---|---|---|
| [CoinGecko](https://www.coingecko.com) | Independent price and market data aggregator | Broad coverage, consistent asset metadata | Price-first layout, ads |
| [CoinMarketCap](https://coinmarketcap.com) | Price and market data aggregator, owned by Binance | The canonical list of assets | Exchange ownership, ads, sponsored placements |
| [CryptoRank](https://cryptorank.io) | Market data, fundraising rounds, token unlocks | Funding and vesting data | Dashboard noise |
| [Coinlib](https://coinlib.io) | Price tracker and portfolio tool | Compact, dense tables | Trading focus |

## Research and project intelligence

| Site | What it is | What we take | What we leave |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Messari](https://messari.io) | Research and data on crypto assets | Structured asset profiles, governance tracking | Paywalls |
| [RootData](https://www.rootdata.com) | Database of projects, investors, and people | Entity relationships: who funded and who built what | Marketing-style project blurbs |

## Onchain analytics

| Site | What it is | What we take | What we leave |
|---|---|---|---|
| [DefiLlama](https://defillama.com) | TVL, fees, and revenue across chains and protocols | Neutral, open data with no ads; a live source we link to for figures | |
| [L2Beat](https://l2beat.com) | Risk analysis of Ethereum layer 2 networks | Precise, sourced technical assessment; the Stages framework | |
| [Token Terminal](https://tokenterminal.com) | Financial metrics for protocols | Revenue and earnings framing | Paywalls |
| [Artemis](https://www.artemis.xyz) | Cross-chain fundamental metrics | Comparable metrics across chains | |
| [Dune](https://dune.com) | Community SQL dashboards on onchain data | Onchain data as a citable source | |
| [growthepie](https://www.growthepie.com) | Ethereum layer 2 metrics | Clean L2 comparisons | |
| [ultrasound.money](https://ultrasound.money) | ETH supply and burn tracker | One subject, done exhaustively | |
