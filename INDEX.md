# Index

All pages in Chainlore.

## Layer 1 blockchains

- [TRON](pages/tron.md): TRX, elected block producers, resources, and stablecoins.
- [BNB Chain](pages/bnb-chain.md): BNB Smart Chain, staking, and Chain Fusion.
- [XRP Ledger](pages/xrp-ledger.md): XRP, validator lists, payments, and exchange.
- [Monero](pages/monero.md): privacy-oriented proof of work, XMR, and tail emission.
- [Bitcoin](pages/bitcoin.md): the first cryptocurrency, a proof-of-work
  peer-to-peer electronic cash system.
- [Ethereum](pages/ethereum.md): a proof-of-stake blockchain for
  general-purpose smart contracts.
- [Hyperliquid](pages/hyperliquid.md): a layer 1 blockchain built around
  an onchain order book for perpetual futures and spot trading.
- [Solana](pages/solana.md): a proof-of-stake blockchain designed for high
  throughput, using Proof of History to order transactions.
- [Terra](pages/terra.md): a blockchain whose algorithmic stablecoin, UST,
  collapsed with its LUNA token in May 2022.

## Layer 2 networks

- [Lightning Network](pages/lightning-network.md): Bitcoin payment channels, routing, and liquidity.
- [Arbitrum](pages/arbitrum.md): a family of optimistic rollups that
  settle to Ethereum.
- [Base](pages/base.md): an Ethereum rollup incubated by Coinbase.
- [Optimism](pages/optimism.md): an optimistic rollup on Ethereum and the
  origin of the OP Stack.

## Stablecoins

- [Stablecoin](pages/stablecoin.md): crypto assets designed to hold a
  fixed value, with a list of stablecoins and their failures.
- [Tether](pages/tether.md): USDT, a US dollar stablecoin issued by
  Tether Limited.
- [USDC](pages/usdc.md): a US dollar stablecoin issued by Circle.

## Decentralised finance

- [Liquidation](pages/liquidation.md): collateral thresholds, auctions,
  forced position closures, and remaining shortfalls.
- [Polymarket](pages/polymarket.md): prediction markets, collateral,
  resolution, and the separate US exchange.
- [Automated market maker](pages/automated-market-maker.md): liquidity pools,
  pricing curves, and liquidity-provider risks.
- [Curve](pages/curve.md): exchange pools, crvUSD, lending, and vote-escrow governance.
- [Ethena](pages/ethena.md): USDe, its backing and hedging, sUSDe, and ENA.
- [MakerDAO and Sky](pages/makerdao-sky.md): the protocol behind Dai and
  USDS, its collateral system, and its governance transition.
- [Uniswap](pages/uniswap.md): a decentralised exchange protocol built on
  automated market makers.
- [Aave](pages/aave.md): a lending and borrowing protocol.
- [Lido](pages/lido.md): a liquid staking protocol whose main product is
  stETH, a token for staked ether.

## Exchanges

- [Bybit](pages/bybit.md): derivatives, custody, the 2025 theft, and regional entities.
- [Binance](pages/binance.md): the exchange, related entities, regulation, and security incidents.
- [Mt. Gox](pages/mt-gox.md): the 2014 collapse and subsequent creditor repayments.
- [Coinbase](pages/coinbase.md): exchange, custody provider, and developer

- [FTX](pages/ftx.md): a cryptocurrency exchange that collapsed and filed

## Legislation

- [CLARITY Act](pages/clarity-act.md): a United States market-structure
  bill that passed the House but stalled in the Senate.
- [GENIUS Act](pages/genius-act.md): a United States law, signed in July
  2025, that regulates payment stablecoins.
- [MiCA](pages/mica.md): the European Union's regulation of crypto-assets
  and stablecoins.
- [Payment Services Act (Japan)](pages/japan-payment-services-act.md):
  the Japanese statute behind its crypto-asset and stablecoin rules.
- [Stablecoins Ordinance (Hong Kong)](pages/hong-kong-stablecoins-ordinance.md):
  Hong Kong's licensing regime for stablecoin issuers.
- [UK cryptoasset regime](pages/uk-cryptoasset-regime.md): the United
  Kingdom's authorisation regime for crypto firms and stablecoins.
