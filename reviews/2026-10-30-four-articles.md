  events, China's historical mining share, price milestones, and the

  court documents, filings, and research. Qualified estimates attributed

  milestones, and recorded the postponed Glamsterdam Hoodi activation.
  Replaced unsupported incident descriptions with the SEC, FBI, and
  original postmortems. Removed unsupported priority claims about funds.
- Arbitrum: corrected Nova's data-availability description, fee allocation,
  challenge timing, retryable-ticket wording, and gaming-program figures.
  Distinguished proposals, votes, transfers, and completed actions.
  Updated Stylus and Security Council incidents with dated primary records.
- Hyperliquid: corrected the small-block gas limit, vault lockup wording,
  token-allocation versus distribution claims, stablecoin milestones,
  and the October 2025 open-interest figures. Attributed funding claims,
  allegations, and analyst explanations. Distinguished announced market
  plans from approved or operational services.

Unsupported details were removed where the consulted sources did not
establish them. Current or disputed claims are attributed and dated.
The Bitcoin and Ethereum articles include Binance's 29–30 October 2026
incident reports; the Arbitrum article includes the associated Security
