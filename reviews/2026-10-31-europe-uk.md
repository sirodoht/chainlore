## Material corrections and current-date additions

- MiCA’s original EMT white-paper notice was wrong: 40 working days concerns intended offering/admission, while the white-paper notice is at least 20 working days before publication.
- Qualified issuer exemptions, safeguarded-fund investment rules, the significant-EMT supervision exception, qualifying non-corporate service providers and transaction-limit methodology. Avoided describing an already mandated technical-standard process as an outstanding future task.
- Replaced the expired 31 August review deadline with the final 30 September date. Added ESMA’s 30 September reform response, 8 October opinion covering all services on non-compliant stablecoins, 14 October USDC classification and 21 October French proposals.
- Replaced secondary descriptions of ESMA’s June wind-down statement and Council’s negotiating position with the opened primary documents. Kept legislation and negotiating proposals distinct.
- Removed the inaccessible Crypto Briefing 2026 claim about Tether’s deliberate non-application. Retained the independently read August 2024 Ardoino interview and attributed criticism.
- Qualified UK pre-2027 regulation so it does not imply that security tokens are outside existing financial-services law. Identified the temporary-registration deadline as the initial deadline.
- Corrected UK safeguarding and issuance descriptions, prevented lending/borrowing from being presented as a separate statutory regulated activity, and updated the missing October perimeter/DeFi consultations.
- Clarified that Bank/FCA supervisory allocation remains proposed; corrected general-business-risk capital versus additional reserves and the systemic redemption clock. Confirmed that the £40 billion guardrail and 70/30 backing policy supersede the former holding-cap and 60/40 proposals.
