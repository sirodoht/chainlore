## Corrections

### CLARITY Act

- Replaced the implication that federal securities/commodities laws had no
  application to crypto before CLARITY with the narrower, supported claim
  that the bill proposes a dedicated market-structure framework.
- Added the official Senate roll call for the 15 September 2026 failed
  cloture vote: 49–50, three-fifths required. Preserved the distinction
  between a motion to proceed and a vote on passage.
- Corrected the 5 October–6 November calendar description: this is a
  non-legislative **state work period**, not a Washington floor-work period.
  Removed the weaker FinTech Weekly citation and its uncorroborated
  post-vote statement/prediction details; retained Orrick's attributed
  assessment of prospects and reconsideration.
- Corrected the Senate ancillary-asset exemption's alternative ceiling:
  10% of the **total dollar value** outstanding at the offering date, not
  10% of token supply. Retained the $50 million/year, four-year and $200
  million aggregate figures, verified in the reported Senate text.
- Included entities **required** to register in the House jurisdiction
  description, the possibility of SEC extensions to the four-year maturity
  timetable, and the actual role of coordinated-control requirements.
- Corrected the section 404 issuer exclusion to permitted issuers and
  OCC-registered foreign issuers, and added the no-substantially-similar-
  past-violation condition to its 90-day good-faith protection.
- Precisely described the September draft's substantial-detrimental-impact
  test for community-bank deposits, and removed the incorrect assertion
  that a statute cannot be reversed. Orrick's relative comparison of
  regulation and legislation is attributed instead.
- Added the missing version-introduction citations. House, Senate Banking,
  and September combined-draft provisions remain explicitly separated.

### GENIUS Act

- Established 18 January 2027 as the Act's effective date, corroborated by
  the OCC final rule, rather than leaving it only as an outside deadline.
- Added the Federal Reserve proposal published 29 September 2026 and the
  Treasury **interim final** state-certification procedure published and
  effective 30 September. Removed the stale assertion that no Treasury
  final rule had been confirmed. Both comment periods end 30 November.
- Tightened the state-regime description: consolidated outstanding
  issuance; committee approval of the regime; cessation of new issuance
  from crossing the threshold when taking that option; and availability
  of federal waivers for continued state-only supervision.
- Corrected and separately cited every reserve-list item. Specified
  on-demand deposits/insured shares, the different repo and reverse-repo
  conditions, and the limited categories eligible in tokenised form.
  The former unrestricted wording wrongly swept tokenised repos into the
  eligible tokenised-assets category.
- Distinguished regulator-authorised incidental activities, included the
  consumer-consent exception to transaction-data restrictions, and added
  the foreign-issuer reciprocity and jurisdictional restrictions.
- Corrected the OCC redemption-notification threshold to each **brand's**
  outstanding issuance, distinguished existing approvals from possible
  pending-application waivers, and preserved the general two-business-day
  redemption limit.
- Added Senator Warren's actual October 2025 Treasury comment as primary
  evidence for her conflict-of-interest criticism. Removed the unnecessary
  inflammatory Naylor quotation. Criticisms remain attributed and the
  Coinbase response to the bank-rewards argument remains present.
- Replaced the unavailable World Liberty secondary reference with OCC
  Corporate Decision 1385. Named the proposed World Liberty Trust Company
  correctly and explicitly distinguished preliminary conditional approval
  from authority to commence business.
- Removed an unavailable signing reference; the actual statute and opened
  Mayer Brown account support enactment. Removed an unnecessary current
  ranking of USDT/USDC and the unsupported 'first' label in the FDIC
  timeline row.
